How quantum mechanics unlocks exponential power — explained without a PhD.

Qubits vs. Bits

A classical bit is 0 or 1. A qubit can be 0, 1, or both at the same time — thanks to superposition. Two qubits explore 4 states simultaneously, growing exponentially.

⚛️ Superposition engine

Quantum Entanglement

Einstein called it "spooky action at a distance." Entangled particles stay connected: measure one, instantly determine the other, no matter the distance. The backbone of quantum teleportation & cryptography.

🔗 Non-locality

Quantum Tunneling

Particles pass through barriers that classical physics says are impossible. This enables flash memory, scanning tunneling microscopes, and even explains nuclear fusion in stars.

⛰️ Through the impossible

Heisenberg's Uncertainty

You can't know both the exact position and momentum of a particle. This isn't a measurement flaw — it's a fundamental property of reality. It also enables unhackable quantum cryptography.
